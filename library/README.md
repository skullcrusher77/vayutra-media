# Photo and video library

Put real footage here and the daily posts will use it automatically as backgrounds for reels, posts and stories.

- `library/photos/`: JPG or PNG photos, at least 1080 px wide.
- `library/clips/`: MP4 videos, 5 to 30 seconds, under 50 MB each. Vertical (9:16) is best, but any shape works.

**Name files by what they show**, using words from the trip, so the right file is picked for each day's theme, e.g.
`konark_surf_1.mp4`, `konark_sun_temple.jpg`, `chilika_boat_ride.mp4`, `oldtown_cycle_ride.jpg`, `puri_beach_sunrise.jpg`,
`students_team_challenge.jpg`, `campfire_reflection.mp4`.

**Only add footage you are allowed to use:**
- your own photos and videos from Vayutra trips (with students' and parents' permission), or
- free stock from Pexels (pexels.com) or Pixabay (pixabay.com), whose licences allow business use without credit.
Never add images from Google search or clips from YouTube or other Instagram accounts: they are copyrighted, and
Instagram mutes, removes or penalises reused content.

**How to add files from your phone:** open github.com/skullcrusher77/vayutra-media, go into `library`, then `photos`
or `clips`, tap **Add file → Upload files**, choose the files and tap **Commit changes**.
